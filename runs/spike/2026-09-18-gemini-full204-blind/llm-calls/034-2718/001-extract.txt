### WHAT TO EXTRACT ###

   - `head` / `tail` MUST exactly match `name` values from `entities`. Direction: head acts on tail.
   - `type`: a short lower-case verb phrase with hyphens (e.g. `attacks`, `uses`, `delivers`, `targets-sector`, `exploits`, `hosts`, `attributed-to`, `affects`, `located-in`, `part-of`, `sends-email-to`).

3. `newRelationTypes`: [{ "name", "definition" }] for relation types you introduced; an empty array is the normal case.

### KNOWN RELATION TYPES ###
- publishes: An organization or vendor officially releases or issues a security advisory, bulletin, or patch.
- addresses-vulnerability-in: A security advisory or patch provides fixes for security vulnerabilities affecting a software product.
- affects: A security vulnerability compromises or impacts a specific software product or component.
- located-in: An organization, entity, or infrastructure is geographically or administratively based in a country or location.
- part-of: An entity or component is a constituent sub-part of a larger system, suite, or organization.
- hosts: An IP address, server, or domain hosts a specific file, malware payload, or service.
- delivers: An exploit, campaign, or delivery mechanism drops or downloads a payload onto a target system.
- exploits: A threat actor, malware, or campaign leverages a specific security vulnerability to compromise systems.
- attacks: A threat actor compromises, targets, or executes unauthorized actions against a system, service, or organization.
